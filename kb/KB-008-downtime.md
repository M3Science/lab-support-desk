# KB-008: LIS downtime: what to do

| Applies to | Typical priority | First owner | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole laboratory | P1 (unplanned) / P4 (planned-downtime questions) | Tier 1 declares, Tier 3 restores | Tier 3 and lab leadership immediately |

## Symptoms
- The LIS is unavailable for multiple users or sites.

## Before you start
- Confirm it is more than one user and more than one workstation.

## Steps
1. Declare downtime per policy and notify lab leadership and the service desk.
2. The lab switches to downtime procedures: downtime labels, paper requisitions, manual result reporting by phone for STAT and critical values.
3. Post updates on a fixed schedule (for example every 30 minutes) until service is restored.
4. After recovery, backload downtime orders and results, and verify a sample against paper records.

## Escalate when
- Immediately. Unplanned downtime is always P1.

## Close the ticket with
Start/end time, cause, number of downtime results backloaded, reconciliation complete Y/N.
