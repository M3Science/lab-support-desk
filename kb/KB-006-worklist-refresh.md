# KB-006: Worklist or pending list not refreshing

| Applies to | Typical priority | First owner | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bench techs | P3 | Tier 1 | Tier 2 (worklist filter build) |

## Symptoms
- Received specimens do not appear on the bench worklist.
- The pending list shows stale specimens that are already resulted.

## Before you start
- Ask whether one workstation or all workstations are affected.

## Steps
1. Refresh the worklist and confirm the filter (section, date range, priority) is correct.
2. Log out and back in to reset the session.
3. Check another workstation. If it works there, clear the local cache per the procedure.
4. Receive a test specimen and confirm it appears.

## Escalate when
- The same specimens are missing on every workstation (Tier 2: filter or routing build).

## Close the ticket with
Workstation, filter used, fix applied, test specimen confirmed Y/N.
